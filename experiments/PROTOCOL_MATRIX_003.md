# MATRIX-003: Conditional environment, transient shocks, and warranted revision

**Protocol frozen on 2026-10-10.** This is implementer-authored synthetic mechanistic investigation, not independently scored psychological identity evaluation. Inputs in `fixtures/matrix_003_frozen_v01.json` are fixed before running the study; each execution records their SHA-256 and exact commit. No prior MATRIX-001/MATRIX-002 scenes are reused.

## Research questions

Previous MATRIX-002 found a mean post-shock field deviation ratio greater than 1 for all five tested profiles. But the shock was encoded in a persistent affect field, so the original test did not actually isolate exogenous removal. New conditional-context architecture supplies explicit, provenance-bound **transient** and **persistent** event classes, together with an independent evidence-bound resolution operation. This experiment separates:

1. **Transient:** a one-tick source pressure which expires. Does its field-policy deviation subsequently contract toward an otherwise matched counterfactual?
2. **Persistent:** sustained context without relief. Does the policy remain different from the no-hazard counterpart? Such persistence could be *correct*; no ideal action label is assumed.
3. **Resolved:** identical sustained event but independently witnessed relief is admitted after four cycles. Does the policy trajectory differ from the continued-hazard arm, and does its distance from the no-hazard arm shrink?
4. **Revision:** same relief plus sign-labeled operator feedback at a fixed forced action; compare developmental target and actual policy decisions to an equal-matched zero-reward branch. Score change alone is not justified learning.

## Controls and plan

Four new situations: marsh signal circuit, botanical glasshouse, astronomical mirror, and museum floor. Eight fixed seeds (13,29,47,83,131,173,239,307). Three profiles: `conditional` (source-bound context and repair gain .12), `drive_only` (same source-bound context without conditional target repair), and unchanged `legacy_v02` without context API (the previous affect-based shock encoding). Five branches per profile and case: no hazard, transient, persistent, resolved, revised. This is 4x8x3x5 = 480 independently checkpoint-restored continuations. Sixteen cognitive decisions/cycles are expected per branch, depending on event schedule.

The environmental event text, seed, source actor, allowed actions and timeline are fixed within each profile. Branches differ only by declared condition operation (and on legacy arm its affect-based encoding), not by access to new memory. In all contexts, an admitted subject-owned lived observation is necessary to introduce a contextual state. Resolution requires a new independent admitted event. Invalid subject archives, missing origin IDs, duplicate context IDs and one source resolving itself are blocked by API tests.

The longitudinal series includes an initial source event, four endogenous cycles, one clearance/report event, and eight more endogenous cycles. The transient source is active for the first post-binding tick only. The persistent source lasts until resolution; the resolved and revised branches admit a documented termination. In the revision branch, the same forced decision is recorded in both revision and resolved comparator so only the explicitly labeled outcome differs; no outside semantic truth is inferred from that numeric feedback. The no-hazard arm receives the same neutral event titles, but no pressure, to support paired comparison.

## Measures

Store all per-branch and per-tick state, source IDs, context regime, policy score vectors, selected decisions and margins. Report the L1 field and policy divergence relative to the no-hazard branch at the first pulse and final pulse, plus paired decline ratio (final/initial). For persistent vs resolved, measure post-resolution final field and policy differences and active source indicators. Report matched decision changes, including whether margins shift without flips.

All comparisons are within profile/scenario/seed. A transience gain can only be claimed operationally if the field approaches the matched no-hazard trajectory after its external driver expires. **Even that is not identity recovery.** Neither a smaller field L1 nor a larger action change automatically implies better behavior.

## Explicit risk controls

No use of real quantum or occult effects. No assertion of human cognition, consciousness or literary authenticity. No route to production The-Doctor-Lives. Semantic social claim admission remains separately blocked from production due to EGREGORE-001. No hyperparameter tuning on these now-public cases followed by a confirmatory claim; any new field coefficients are part of this pre-frozen implementation and all regressions/nulls must be retained.

The `legacy_v02` arm cannot be considered perfectly input-equivalent at the *representation* level: it encodes disturbance as persisting affect whereas the new controller has an expiring external-context channel. It is an architectural comparison, not a blinded causal ablation of a single parameter. The appropriate strict causal comparison is `conditional` vs `drive_only` with the same source evidence and identical checkpoint state semantics except the registered repair gain.

Publication requires executable case-level evidence, raw hashes, CPU and checkpoint integrity checks, full failures, and a clear separation between synthetic functionality and independently adjudicated identity continuity.
