## Research question

Which subsystems of the Noetic Engine v0.2 actually affect its scored decisions under matched input histories, and which expected null mechanisms remain neutral? The assay tests causal leverage under deterministic software interventions. It **does not** establish character authenticity, cognition comparable to humans, advantage against other agent architectures, verified autobiographical memory, or superior long-range personality coherence.

The primary contrast is the complete opt-in research profile against mechanism-specific ablations. The full profile activates bounded fast state, source-reviewed social attribution, source-reviewed self hypotheses, prospective intentions, learned symbols, bounded developmental regulation, synthetic recurrent dynamics, and isolated Oracle questions. All conditions use the identical source events, source-review decisions, forced early training action, outcome reward, action eligibility, chronology and scenario content. No simulated events or social inference is admitted as canonical autobiography.

## Dataset and method

The fixture `experiments/fixtures/longitudinal_matrix_v01.json` is frozen by exact SHA-256 in each output. It contains four synthetic situations: witness duty, scientific compulsion, relational betrayal, and divided loyalty. Each situation undergoes nine identical types of source events, plus two endogenous cycles, with eight distinct seeds (7, 19, 41, 73, 101, 163, 227, 311).

A source-owned witnessed quotation supports one synthetic *attributed* social claim. A distinct, source-linked self-model hypothesis and an intention are established after the first event. An arbitrary symbol is associated with that witnessed event. The second substantive event constrains the decision to `investigate`, so all arms receive identical operator-scored feedback on the same action. This is **not verified environmental reward**. Later events include an uncertain third-party report and a conflict involving a witness.

The subject and provenance are kept distinct. The third-party report is retained as a claim but an attempted direct policy admission must fail. Source quotation checks and operator review remain enabled across every condition. No condition gets permission to bypass those safeguards.

## Prespecified conditions

The 13 conditions are full, original v0.1-style controller, affect-only control, removal of intentions, removal of relationships, removal of reviewed social beliefs, removal of self-model, removal of developmental field, removal of synthetic recurrent field, removal of homeostatic regulator, removal of cue association, removal of Oracle, and replacing the 'amber-sigil' label with an unrelated neutral key. This is not a fully crossed factorial design. It is a matched **single-family removal matrix**, supplemented by two complete simplifications and a lexical-key negative control.

Removing the Oracle should produce no change in action scores or choices because Oracle conjectures are noncanonical and presently have no policy input. Neutral-key substitution should also have no effect because the label has no intrinsic meaning. Those two exact null predictions are enforced programmatically. The number of speculative questions may still differ for Oracle removal.

## Primary measures

For each source-aligned step, record all action total scores, selected policy, three field values, named causal family pressures, eligible actions, source IDs, attribution IDs and oracle state. For each scenario/seed pair compute mean L1 difference of the aligned action-score vectors, mean field L1 difference and percentage of steps with a different action. Compute these quantities separately for each ablation. Retain full per-condition, per-step traces; do not publish only averaged successes.

Descriptive paired bootstrap intervals use 1,500 draws, seed 2026, over the 32 synthetic scenario/seed pairs. Because the cases are manually authored and the outcomes are not independently labeled, these intervals describe only this synthetic fixture distribution. They are **not** population confidence intervals, clinical conclusions, or proof of identity quality. They are not for significance-driven promotion decisions.

## Predicted observations and falsification

The original intention pressure has an explicit positive contribution to the relevant tendency. At least one scenario should show nonzero score change when intentions are disabled. That tests software causal engagement only. Removing the recurrent or developmental component may alter policy trajectories, but its having an effect is not evidence of a *beneficial* effect. A system that is highly labile or pathologically rigid can pass a simple lesion-change test.

If the Oracle and neutral symbol arms change action scores, the architectural null assumptions are violated. If a third-party archive claim enters policy without witnessed source review, the provenance boundary is broken. If scores differ because of unmatched allowed actions or learning feedback, the comparison is invalid and the runner fails.
