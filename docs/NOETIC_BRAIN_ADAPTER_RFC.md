# RFC 002: Review-before-code boundary for Noetic to Pretorius

**Status: REQUEST FOR REVIEW. NOT APPROVED. NOT IMPLEMENTED.**
Target: `Azimn/The-Doctor-Lives`, definitive production Pretorius.

This RFC is a constraint specification, NOT executable `NoeticBrainAdapter`.
It responds to the auditor requirement that the adapter be reviewed before its
first line of integration code exists.

## Strict architectural compatibility

The-Doctor-Lives has `PretoriusBrainPort`, an already causally load-bearing
recurrent substrate, signed retrieval-time Persona Connectome activation, UPPB
protected evidence, and canonical snapshot provenance. Its `ARCHITECTURE_CONTRACT.md`
forbids introducing another independent identity store, competing recurrent
neural brain, planner, or foreign autobiographical authority.

The Noetic Engine shall not be mounted alongside Pretorius as a second brain.
The only permissible route is **donor-mechanism transfer** to already authorized
surfaces after independent causal evaluation. In particular, Noetic's synthetic
recurrent network must NOT run as a second actor inside production Pretorius.
If its learning rule earns promotion, adapt the rule to the existing
`PretoriusRecurrentSubstrate` under a versioned migration. The new subsystem
may not replace the ten-action vocabulary or policy-decision provenance.

## Candidate transferred mechanisms

A narrowly reviewed contribution could be a bounded source-attributed belief
pressure, a counterfactual-question quarantine, or a slow self-model tendency
update. Each must be lesioned independently in a cloned production state.
All new pressure terms need per-family caps and must not duplicate already
active state-to-policy bridge contributions. Strong surface-style resemblance is
not enough; changed policy scores and matched action outcomes are required.

## Source and memory mapping

Noetic's `lived_observed` is NOT automatically equivalent to production
`lived_runtime_memory`. Historical records remain split into canonical,
reconstructed, and admitted synthesized classes. Explicit source authority,
admission status, version, subject and SHA-256 must be verified by the production
`EvidenceAuthority` interface, not by caller strings or file paths.

No Noetic sandbox observations, synthetic simulations, Oracle questions,
unverified self-hypotheses, third-party memories, or prototype neural weights may
be transplanted as production Pretorius autobiography. The 70-node,
243-edge Persona Connectome is protected and never rewritten as neural truth.

## Proposed no-code port mapping

Input: an approved, already verified production evidence reference plus a
bounded snapshot of eligible felt/interoceptive, relationship, concern,
commitment and existing neural-policy contributions.

Candidate calculation: a pure, deterministic function returning bounded signed
delta scores, supporting source IDs, confidence, causal component name, policy
version and explicit abstention. It receives **no** mutation rights.

Policy integration: only the currently authorized Pretorius policy bridge may
consume the candidate deltas after independent matched tests demonstrate
incremental benefit. It must preserve original network scores, intermediate
pressure terms, selected mode, source IDs and checkpoint SHA-256.

Outcome: the production store alone decides whether a lived consequence is
admissible and whether plasticity may occur. Noetic receives no right to
self-approve facts or to call tools through the character.

## Required preimplementation approvals

First, reconcile exact production port methods and release candidate head with
the live `ARCHITECTURE_CONTRACT.md`. Second, obtain assessor review of the
proposed method, source boundaries, and fallback. Third, reproduce a
state-identical lesion/neutral-key/fresh-decoder comparison on independently
prepared fixtures. Fourth, specify migrations and rollback preserving all
canonical and subjective state. Fifth, require a versioned opt-in config that
leaves accepted v0.4 behavior unchanged by default.

No full adapter implementation, signature compatibility claim, merge, or
production integration is authorized by this RFC. A later approval must cite
the exact reviewed commits and measured results.

## Safety and audit consequences

Any source-manifest mismatch, stale state schema, new future schema, missing
policy trace, archive subject swap, hallucinated quotation, or unauthorized
action fails closed. Resulting audit traces must distinguish renderer output
from cognitive selection and preserve both negative and positive cases.
