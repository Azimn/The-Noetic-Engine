## Execution and scope

47 regression tests passed. The experiment completed 416 deterministic trajectories: 13 conditions times four synthetic scenarios times eight fixed seeds. Each of 12 comparators had 32 matched cases and 352 aligned cognitive decisions/cycles, for 384 paired case comparisons in all. Full case-level traces, original source event classes, reviewer decisions, policy contributions, chosen actions, and experiment configurations were emitted. No language model renderer or external human adjudication participated.

The complete research profile was compared against isolated removals and two broad simplified baselines. The second substantive observation forced the same investigate action across conditions before all conditions received the same operator-assigned feedback. This controls one learning-source confound but does **not** imply the reward represented a verified real-world success.

## Interpretation

The coupled controller is measurably sensitive to prospective intentions and its regulatory path. In this synthetic program, disabling either the developmental layer or regulator changed 21 selected actions, and disabling intentions changed 29. These are measurements of **implementation-level leverage**, not better character development or more accurate behavior.

The sparse recurrent field changed scores substantially more often than it changed discrete decisions. With recurrent dynamics removed, 3 of 352 selections changed. That does **not** yet demonstrate an improved or identity-bearing neural substrate. A fresh-decoder donor audit, stronger competing non-neural baseline, independent held-out outcome labels and long-horizon comparisons remain necessary.

The Egregore, despite source-reviewed attributed-belief scores, changed no selected actions in these scenarios. The cue system similarly changed scores but not decisions. These are candidates for stronger causal testing, not grounds for increasing arbitrary weights until positive-looking results appear. Trialing larger weights on this public fixture would contaminate confirmatory interpretation and must be labeled exploratory only.

The v0.1-style controller differed from the combined v0.2 system in 13 of 352 choices. No arm has proven superiority because the test establishes no independently correct action. The changes could reflect improvement, harmful drift or simply a different arbitrary weight design.

## Integrity and analytical limitations

All contexts are author-generated; four scenarios share one structural event sequence and eight seeds are not independent human behavioral samples. Consequently, even the reported 95% paired bootstrap intervals in the raw aggregate are **descriptive resampling of this fixed synthetic matrix**, not population inference. Review judgments are programmatically assigned and are not authenticated people. The attribution gate verifies exact quotations, not the semantics of the claimed mental-state predicate. Source retrieval remains lexical and does not verify entailment. No real model swaps, semantic memory checks, long-run justified learning, normative action metrics, or gold-standard identity-trajectory comparisons were performed.

We retain source negative constraints: arbitrary neutral keys do as much as Gothic labels under this method; social source swaps and false autobiographical admission remain risks; lexical cue success is not paraphrase comprehension; neural score changes do not localize a durable Pretorius phenotype.

## Decision and next experimental gate

Treat MATRIX-001 as **a measured, limited diagnostic result** suitable for selecting future research questions, not as acceptance of the full Noetic identity thesis. Preserve nulls unchanged. The next independently frozen matrix must ask whether coupling improves correctness and justified developmental change compared with strong prompt-only, retrieval-only and static bridge baselines, with adjudicated scenes, model-family transfers and explicit false-memory penalties.

Continue Egregore adversarial source-meaning tests and Oracle utility tests under evidence quarantine. Preserve the independent `NoeticBrainAdapter` review gate and the unresolved neural donor E4 fresh-decoder work. No production promotion is justified by this experiment.
