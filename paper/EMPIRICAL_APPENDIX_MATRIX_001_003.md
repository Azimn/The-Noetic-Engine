# Empirical appendix A: synthetic Noetic diagnostics MATRIX-001–003

**Status:** original repository results audit, 2026-10-10. These are *engineering experiments on author-generated cases*, not independent scientific demonstrations of character identity or consciousness. Prepared as an appendix candidate to `paper/NOETIC_FIELD_PAPER_V2.md`; the conceptual prospectus is not rewritten to claim efficacy.

## What has actually been executed

MATRIX-001: 13 controller conditions x 4 author-written situations x 8 seeds = 416 trajectories. Frozen synthetic fixture, 47 passing tests. Removing persistent intentions changed 29/352 paired actions. Removing developmental feedback changed 21/352; removing the recurrent substrate changed 3/352; removing Egregore or symbolic cues changed zero selected actions. The Oracle isolation and neutral-key substitution were exactly zero in score and selection. These are policy-level ablations, not behavioral correctness measurements.

MATRIX-002: 5 architecture profiles x 4 fresh author-written situations x 8 seeds x 5 checkpoint continuations = 800 branches, 52 passing tests. The original v0.2 full controller's mean final/initial matched shock field L1 was 1.9929 (>1) and none of the tested profiles averaged below 1. Opposite signed rewards altered weights and slow targets but did not change selected held-out actions. Crucial confound: the one-time shock remained in stored affect and did not undergo full context termination, so that experiment did not measure recovery after a truly expired external cause.

MATRIX-003: 3 architecture profiles x 4 further author-written situations x 8 seeds x 5 checkpoint continuations = 480 branches, 62 passing tests. A versioned and source-linked environmental-context register distinguishes active transient input from a persistent hazard and separate observed resolution. In the new conditional controller, average end/initial field L1 ratio for an expired transient cause was 0.000205628; in matched source-drive-only control 0.001033275. The legacy persistent-affect encoding was 1.386140021, a representation-mismatched comparator that should not be used for a single-parameter superiority claim. Under active hazards the field remained distinct; after separate resolution the difference contracted. However, operator feedback changed slow targets and scores without changing any selected actions.

EGREGORE-001 (separate causal integrity audit): in 15 authored adversarial samples, source quotation/speaker/hearsay protections rejected their intended integrity failures, but six semantically contradictory, verbatim source-backed social beliefs were all approved after a deliberately incorrect synthetic reviewer annotation. All six affected policy scores and two changed action selection. This demonstrates a live quote-to-meaning confabulation risk despite correct source-span checking. Private other-mind beliefs were not and cannot be directly verified by the model.

## Theory adjustments required

1. The original morphogenetic analogy must now specify **environment-conditioned target organization**. Return to a previous field state is not inherently desirable when circumstances justify a new goal. Distinguish source persistence, affect residue, policy state and slow developmental targets.
2. The bounded field is presently an engineering state regulator; calling its deterministic contraction regeneration of identity would overstate the evidence.
3. Prospective intention has stronger demonstrated action leverage than the optional synthetic recurrent substrate in MATRIX-001; both may still harm actual decision quality. Nothing yet validates a recovered Pretorius recurrent phenotype; the historical donor E4 fresh-decoder gate remains open.
4. Egregore should be considered an *attributed subject interpretation*, not an observed third-party mind state. Quotation presence and a named review actor are insufficient to authorize semantic truth.
5. In all three MATRIX tests, **selected action change under reward was absent** where specifically measured. Future designs must measure the correctness of consequential choices and justified target revision under independent labels, rather than demanding arbitrary action flips.
